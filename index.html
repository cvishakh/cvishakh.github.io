Vishakh Cheruparambath

I'm a Master's student in Electromobility (ACES) program at Friedrich-Alexander-Universität Erlangen-Nürnberg, currently writing my master's thesis at EOS GmbH on vision-transformer based classification of spare parts. My interests lie at the intersection of applied AI, data engineering, and process automation, focusing on agentic systems and automated workflows that make industrial processes faster and more reliable.

Alongside my studies I have worked at EOS and Siemens Mobility, where I built AI agents, automated workflows, and turned unstructured data into usable insight. I began my career on the helicopter shop floors of Hindustan Aeronautics Limited in Bangalore, India. Prior to that, I earned a Bachelor of Technology in Mechanical Engineering from APJ Abdul Kalam Technological University in India.

Feel free to write to me for any inquiries related to my work or general questions.
